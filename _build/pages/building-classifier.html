The challenge

The MSU AI Club's Campus Vision Challenge asked teams to identify which campus building appears in a photo, across ten buildings: Butler, Carpenter, Lee, McCain, McCool, Simrall, Swalm and Walker Halls, Old Main, and the Student Union. Judges ran each team's model on their own held-out photos, so the model had to cope with whatever lighting, angle and phone camera the judges used.

Approach

  • Backbone. An ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50 with its final layer replaced by a ten-way classifier, fine-tuned end to end with SGD (learning rate 0.001, momentum 0.9). We tried EfficientNet first, but ResNet-50 did better on this task.
  • Augmentation aimed at the judges' photos. Resize to 580 and random-crop to 512, colour jitter for time of day, horizontal flips, occasional small rotations and vertical flips, Gaussian blur for shaky or out-of-focus shots, and random erasing so the model cannot depend on any single feature such as one doorway or sign.
  • Robust data loading. A custom image-folder loader skips corrupt files instead of crashing a training run, which mattered on limited Colab sessions.
  • Judging. A standalone inference script runs on GPU or CPU, loads the trained weights and prints a prediction and confidence for every image in a folder.
