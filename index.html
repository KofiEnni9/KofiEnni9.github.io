Selected work

  • OREAM: end-to-end off-road planning from camera and LiDAR

    CAVS research, 2026, lead developer. Paper in preparation for SPIE.

    A TransFuser-based planner that scores a vocabulary of candidate trajectories, built on 2019 and 2020 rosbags whose sensors first had to be made to line up.

    Read the deep dive

  • Measuring tree trunks from a single LiDAR scan

    CAVS research, 2026. C++17, PCL, Eigen.

    Ground removal, a breast-height slice, Euclidean clustering on a hand-written k-d tree, and RANSAC plus least-squares fits, profiled stage by stage.

    Read the deep dive

  • Semantic segmentation of off-road LiDAR

    CAVS research, 2024 to 2025. FRNet, Point Transformer V3, SPVCNN.

    Labelling every return as grass, shrubs, foliage, smooth or rough trail, or obstacle: 0.65 mIoU on held-out scans and 0.94 IoU on high vegetation.

    Read the deep dive

  • BusyBox: a benchmark for affordance generalization

    Microsoft Research, 2025. Co-author, accepted at CoRL.

    A rearrangeable board of switches, sliders, buttons and dials for testing whether VLA policies understand the controls they touch. I automated data collection and operated the robot.

    Read the deep dive

  • Marionette Live: driving a robot arm with your own arm

    HackGT 13, Georgia Tech, 2026. Team of three.

    Webcam pose tracking drives an SO-101 arm in real time, with per-joint filtering, exact URDF-fitted kinematics and a safety state machine for when tracking is lost.

    Read the deep dive

  • SALO: a PDF reader that reads along with you

    Personal project, 2025. Electron, React, FastAPI.

    Streams a summary of the page in view and answers questions across open PDFs, using hybrid vector and BM25 retrieval and Claude tool calls.

    Read the deep dive

  • Danso: an adaptive study partner for Ghana's NSMQ

    Personal project, 2024. FAISS, Mistral-7B, LinUCB.

    Retrieves real past quiz questions, explains them with a 4-bit open model, and uses a contextual bandit to choose what to practise next.

    Read the deep dive

  • Recognising campus buildings from a photo 1st place

    MSU AI Club Campus Vision Challenge, 2024.

    A fine-tuned ResNet-50 that tells ten Mississippi State buildings apart at 99.45% validation accuracy.

    Read the deep dive
