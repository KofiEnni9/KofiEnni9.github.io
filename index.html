Research

Oct 2024 – now

Undergraduate Researcher, Center for Advanced Vehicular Systems

Mississippi State University, Starkville, MS

  • Leading development of OREAM, an end-to-end off-road planning model in PyTorch and CUDA. A TransFuser-based camera and LiDAR fusion backbone proposes multiple anchor-based candidate trajectories, and the model picks one by combining a learned drivability cost, a LiDAR traversability cost, and a destination cost. I built the data and evaluation pipelines over camera and LiDAR driving logs.
  • Benchmarking multi-trajectory planning heads, from winner-takes-all multi-mode decoders (MTP-style) to vocabulary-scoring heads (VADv2 and Hydra-MDP-style), and integrating the planner into the MAVS autonomy stack to evaluate cross-season generalization, runtime, and accuracy against existing traversability models.
  • Built a C++17 LiDAR pipeline that estimates tree-trunk diameter from off-road point clouds collected on a Warthog UGV: elevation-grid ground removal, a hand-written k-d tree for Euclidean clustering, and RANSAC plus least-squares circle fitting with PCL and Eigen, profiled stage by stage for latency.
  • Contributed to NATURE, CAVS's open-source C++ ROS and ROS 2 autonomy stack for off-road navigation, extending its elevation-grid perception module and point-cloud interfaces.
  • Adapting NVIDIA AlpaSim, an open-source closed-loop autonomous-vehicle simulator, to evaluate off-road driving policies.
  • Trained a FrustumNet model for 7-class semantic segmentation of off-road LiDAR (vegetation, trail types, grass, obstacles) on an expanded 450-scan labeled dataset, reaching 0.65 mIoU on held-out scans and 0.94 IoU on high vegetation.
